Family Funeral Cover

When the worst happens, your family won't carry it alone.

Why it matters

A funeral brings the whole family together, and the costs arrive quickly: the coffin, transport, the funeral house and feeding mourners. Family funeral cover pays out in cash, so your family can focus on saying goodbye instead of raising money.

Who it is for

  • Individuals and their immediate family
  • Extended family, including parents and parents-in-law
  • Employers offering funeral benefits to staff
  • Churches, associations and savings groups
  • Small businesses and cooperatives
Types of cover

What we can arrange

Additional benefits

Options such as transport and repatriation of the deceased, a cash support benefit for the household, and memorial cover, depending on the insurer.

Common questions

Questions about family cover

What is a waiting period?

The time after you join before a natural death is covered, often several months. Accidental death is often covered from the start. We show you each plan's waiting period before you join.

Can I cover my parents?

Yes. Most plans let you add parents and parents-in-law, usually for an extra premium that depends on age.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Most plans have a short grace period. Contact us straight away and we will help you keep your cover in force.

Can our church or company get a group plan?

Yes. Group schemes are often cheaper per member. We design the scheme, compare insurers and help run it.
