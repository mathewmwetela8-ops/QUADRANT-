Common questions

Questions about property cover

What is under-insurance?

If you insure for less than the real value, the insurer may reduce any claim in proportion. Correct values protect your claim.

Should I insure my house at market value?

No. Insure for the full cost of rebuilding, which can be higher or lower than the market price.

Are my solar panels and generator covered?

They can be, if they are declared and included. Tell us about them and we will make sure they are listed.

Does buildings cover include my contents?

No. Buildings and contents are separate covers. Many clients take both on one policy.
