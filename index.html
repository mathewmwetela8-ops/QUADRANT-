Who we are

A broker that works for you

Quadrant Insurance Brokers is a Zambian insurance broker that puts the client at the centre of four core services: motor, family funeral, liability and property insurance. We work for our clients, not for insurers. We compare cover from insurers licensed by the Pensions and Insurance Authority, recommend what fits, and stand with our clients through every claim.
