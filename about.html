Our story

Four founders, one whole

Quadrant was founded in Lusaka by four partners: two insurance professionals with [XX] years of combined experience in the Zambian market, and two specialists in marketing and programme management. Between us we have placed cover, handled claims, built brands and run large programmes. We saw the same problem from every side: people buy insurance they do not fully understand, and find out what it really covers only when they claim.

We started Quadrant to fix that. A broker's job is to sit on the client's side of the table. We explain options in plain language, compare insurers honestly, and do the hard work when something goes wrong.

The Quadrant model

The client is number one, at the centre

Our logo is our business model. The client sits at the centre. Around them are the four quadrants of cover we arrange, each with its own colour. Every conversation starts in the middle, with the client, and works outward to the cover they need.

  • Motor: Cover for private cars, taxis and minibuses, trucks and business fleets, compared across Zambia's licensed insurers.
  • Family: Funeral plans for you, your spouse, children and extended family, plus group schemes for employers, churches and associations.
  • Liabilities: Public, products, professional, employers' and directors' liability for businesses, professionals and organisations.
  • Property: Buildings, contents and business property against fire, theft, storm and flood, including business interruption.

Mission

We put clients at the centre of everything we do. We compare Zambia's licensed insurers on every angle that matters, explain each option in plain language, and stand with our clients from the first quote to the final claim.

Our values

Customer centricity, trust and reliability

Three values guide every decision, from the first quote to the final claim.

1

Client at the Centre

Every recommendation starts with the client's needs, budget and circumstances, not with what is easiest to sell.

Customer centricity is the first value because it is the centre of our logo. Before we quote, we listen. We look at the whole picture across motor, family, liability and property, so that nothing important is missed and nothing unnecessary is sold.

In practice
  • We listen before we quote.
  • We speak plainly, in the language our client is most comfortable with.
  • We look at the client's whole picture across all four quadrants.
  • We measure ourselves by claims paid and clients who stay.
2

Trust

We earn trust by being honest, even when honesty costs us a sale.

Insurance is bought on trust and tested at claim time. We tell clients what a policy does not cover as clearly as what it does, we are open about how we are paid, and we only place cover with insurers licensed by the Pensions and Insurance Authority.

In practice
  • We explain exclusions, excesses and waiting periods before anyone signs.
  • We are open about how we are paid.
  • We protect our clients' information and keep it confidential.
  • We only place cover with PIA-licensed insurers.
3

Reliability

We are there when it matters most, and that is at claim time.

A policy is only as good as the service behind it. We answer quickly, keep our clients informed, and follow every claim through to settlement. Renewals are never left to chance, so cover never lapses by accident.

In practice
  • We respond within one working day.
  • We follow every claim through to settlement.
  • We remind clients before renewal so cover never lapses.
  • We do what we say, when we say we will.
