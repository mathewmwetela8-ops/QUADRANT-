QUADRANT · 2027 operational plan

Insurers, compliance and claims

Trust and reliability, built into the routine

Insurer panel

5 PIA-licensed insurers by 31 January, 7 to 8 by May. Chosen on claims record first (30%), then strength, product fit, service and terms.

Performance reviews in May, August and November.

Compliance

Client premiums in a separate account, two signatories, reconciled monthly.

AML, data protection and complaints policies from January. Every PIA return on time.

Claims

Reported to the insurer the same working day. Weekly updates until settlement.

Escalation after 5 working days. Rainy-season and festive readiness every year.
